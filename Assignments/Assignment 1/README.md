# Assignment 1: The Architectural Gap
### The Problem
The goal was to explore the fundamental differences between the dense, matrix-multiplication-heavy design of AI and the sparse, event-driven efficiency of the human brain.

### My Approach
I wrote a dialogue between a holographic brain and an AI server. This allowed me to contrast the brain's 20-watt efficiency with the AI's massive power requirements, focusing on "continuous learning" versus "offline retraining".

### Results & Meaning
The assignment concluded that intelligence is not just about the scale of data, but the "elegance of constraints". Selective processing—firing only when meaningful change is detected—is the key to closing the gap between AI and biological agents.
