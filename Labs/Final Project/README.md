# Final Project: Bio-Inspired AI Audit (ChatGPT-4o)
### What I Did
I conducted a comprehensive "audit" of OpenAI’s GPT-4o through the lens of computational neuroscience. I ran experiments covering World Models, Predictive Coding, Sensory Processing, Episodic Memory, and Executive Attention.

### What I Learned
I discovered that while ChatGPT is a "Super-Semantic" simulation with perfect working memory, it lacks 3D Reference Frames for viewpoint-invariant reasoning and the Multisensory Binding needed for a unified experience. 

### Challenges Faced
The main challenge was designing experiments to prove a negative—identifying the "Inattentional Blindness" and lack of spatial perception in a system that is designed to sound authoritative on all subjects.
