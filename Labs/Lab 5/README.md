# Lab 05: Sensory Processing & AI Perception
### What I Did
In this lab, I explored how biological systems transform raw physical energy—like light and sound—into internal representations. I performed experiments comparing human sensory thresholds and noise filtering to the way artificial neural networks process raw data. I specifically looked at feature extraction and how biological "bottlenecks" differ from AI data intake.

### What I Learned
I learned that perception is not a passive recording but an active construction. The brain uses hierarchical processing to turn simple edges into complex objects. Comparing this to AI showed me that while a model can "see" pixels, it often lacks the biological ability to prioritize stimuli based on survival or environmental saliency.

### Challenges Faced
The biggest challenge was understanding the mathematical mapping between biological sensory organs and their digital counterparts. It was difficult to simulate the "noise" of a biological system in a way that truly reflected how neurons filter out irrelevant background information.
